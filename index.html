What we are here for

KindPath Collective is free, broad-spectrum advocacy and research on Bundjalung Country. Community authority comes before programs.

We are not a crisis service. If you need help today, these services can help right now.

Four service lines

  • Free social advocacy

    Free social advocacy via the Life-Field tool. No NDIS plan is needed for this advocacy.

  • NDIS Specialist Support Coordination

    Specialist Support Coordination. Registration is still in progress, as noted below.

  • KindPath Succession

    Mentoring for people about to leave prison.

  • Social Impact Consultancy

    Social impact consultancy, alongside the advocacy and research.

KindPath Collective Inc is building toward NDIS registration. Until that registration is complete, supports are delivered under our founders’ own sole-trader arrangements.

More on how we work

How we work

  • One steady presence

    When services change and systems don’t talk to each other, we stay. Free advocacy, no NDIS plan needed.

  • Private — your information stays yours

    We only share what you consent to, with who you consent to, and nothing else.

  • You’re in charge, always

    Every plan starts from what matters to you — not the paperwork.

How people reach us

We keep our caseload small on purpose, so we can stay with each person for the long haul. That means we work by referral: most people come to us through a support worker, GP, coordinator, local service, family member or carer.
