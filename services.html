How we help

KindPath doesn’t specialise in one system. We stay with people across the whole journey — through NDIS, health, housing, family and community services — as one consistent presence when everything else feels fragmented. Specialist services do the specialist work; we make sure the person isn’t lost between them.

We work by referral and keep our caseload deliberately small. How referrals work.

Four service lines

  • Free social advocacy

    Free social advocacy via the Life-Field tool. No NDIS plan is needed for this advocacy.

  • NDIS Specialist Support Coordination

    Specialist Support Coordination. Registration is still in progress, as noted below.

  • KindPath Succession

    Mentoring for people about to leave prison.

  • Social Impact Consultancy

    Social impact consultancy, alongside the advocacy and research.

KindPath Collective Inc is building toward NDIS registration. Until that registration is complete, supports are delivered under our founders’ own sole-trader arrangements.
