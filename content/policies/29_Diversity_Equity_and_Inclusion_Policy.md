**KindPath Collective Inc**

# Diversity, Equity and Inclusion Policy

Version 1.0 | Effective: 29 August 2026 | Review: 29 August 2027 | Owner: Director

## 1. Purpose

To ensure KindPath’s services and workplace are genuinely accessible and welcoming to people regardless of disability, race, culture, gender identity, sexuality, religion, age, or socioeconomic background.

## 2. Legislative and standards basis

Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth); Sex Discrimination Act 1984 (Cth); Racial Discrimination Act 1975 (Cth); Age Discrimination Act 2004 (Cth); Anti-Discrimination Act 1977 (NSW).

## 3. Policy statement

KindPath does not discriminate in who it supports or employs, and actively works to remove barriers — physical, communication, financial, or cultural — that would otherwise exclude someone from accessing support. This connects directly to the Individual Values, Beliefs and Cultural Safety Policy for participant-facing practice, and extends it to KindPath’s own workforce and governance.

## 4. Procedure

1. Recruitment and volunteer engagement are open and non-discriminatory, assessed on ability to do the role safely and well.
2. Communication with participants and families is adapted to need (easy-read, interpreter engagement, alternative formats) rather than assuming one format works for everyone.
3. Any complaint of discrimination — by a participant, worker, or member of the public — is treated as seriously as a safeguarding concern.

## 5. Roles and responsibilities

* **Director:** ensures this policy is reflected in recruitment, service design, and communication formats.
