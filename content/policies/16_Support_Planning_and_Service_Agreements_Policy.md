**KindPath Collective Inc**

# Support Planning and Service Agreements Policy

Version 1.0 | Effective: 29 August 2026 | Review: 29 August 2027 | Owner: Director

## 1. Purpose

To ensure every participant has a clear, co-designed support plan and a service agreement that accurately reflects what will be delivered, at what cost, and under what terms.

## 2. Legislative and standards basis

NDIS Practice Standards, Module 3: Provision of Supports — Support planning; Service agreements. NDIS Act 2013 (Cth) and Rules on service agreements and pricing.

## 3. Policy statement

No participant receives ongoing support without a service agreement they understood before signing (or, if verbal/informal by the participant’s choice, a documented record of terms discussed and agreed). Support plans translate NDIS plan goals into concrete, actionable steps — see the goal/action plan approach already used for at least one participant, which is the model for all future participants (per the Director’s stated intent to extend this to everyone KindPath supports).

## 4. Procedure

1. **Intake** gathers what’s needed to plan support safely and effectively (see Phase 2 intake documentation) without over-collecting.
2. **Draft plan and agreement** developed with the participant, in accessible language, covering: goals, specific supports to be delivered, frequency, cost per NDIS price guide (or private rate, clearly labelled as such and separate from NDIS-funded items), start date, review date, and how to end the agreement.
3. **Explanation, not just signature.** The participant (with a support person if wanted) has the agreement explained in plain language and genuinely has the chance to ask questions and negotiate terms before signing.
4. **Goal/action plans.** For each NDIS goal the participant wants KindPath’s help with, a specific action plan is developed with the participant setting out concrete steps, timeframes, and how progress will be tracked — modelled on the existing goal/action plan approach.
5. **Review.** Service agreements and support plans are reviewed at minimum every 12 months, at the participant’s plan review, or sooner if the participant’s circumstances or goals change.

## 5. Roles and responsibilities

* **Director:** ensures every active participant has a current, signed (or otherwise documented) agreement and plan.
* **Workers:** deliver support consistent with the agreed plan; raise it with the Director if delivery is drifting from what was agreed.
