**KindPath Collective Inc**

# Records Retention and Disposal Schedule

Version 1.0 | Effective: 29 August 2026 | Review: 29 August 2027 | Owner: Director

## 1. Purpose

To set out how long different categories of KindPath records are kept, and how they are securely disposed of once that period ends.

## 2. Legislative and standards basis

NDIS Act 2013 (Cth) and Rules (record-keeping obligations for registered providers); Privacy Act 1988 (Cth); HRIP Act 2002 (NSW); Associations Incorporation Act 2009 (NSW) — financial and meeting records.

## 3. Retention schedule

| Record category | Minimum retention | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participant support records (case notes, support plans, ABC data) | 7 years from last support provided (longer if participant was a minor at the time — until they turn 25) | NDIS record-keeping practice; HRIP Act health record standard |
| Incident and reportable incident records | 7 years | NDIS Commission expectations |
| Complaints records | 7 years | NDIS Practice Standards |
| Service agreements and consent records | 7 years from agreement end | Contractual/legal limitation periods |
| Worker screening and induction records | For duration of engagement plus 7 years | Employment/WHS record-keeping |
| Financial records | 7 years | Associations Incorporation Act 2009 (NSW); Australian Taxation Office requirements |
| Governance records (minutes, registers) | Permanently, or minimum 7 years | Associations Incorporation Act 2009 (NSW) |
| Conflict of interest disclosures | Permanently, or minimum 7 years | Governance standard |

## 4. Disposal

* Paper records are shredded, not placed in general waste.
* Digital records are securely deleted (not just removed from an active folder view) using a method that prevents recovery, once the retention period has passed and no legal hold applies.
* A participant’s request for earlier deletion is honoured where no legal retention obligation requires KindPath to keep the record.

## 5. Roles and responsibilities

* **Director:** oversees retention compliance and disposal; maintains a simple log of what’s been disposed of and when.
